Business Data Analyst (fictional example posting)

Responsibilities
- Build and maintain dashboards in Tableau or Power BI for operations and finance teams.
- Write complex SQL queries to extract and analyse data from our data warehouse.

Qualifications
- 2+ years of experience as a data analyst.
- Advanced Excel skills.
- Experience with Python or R for data analysis is a plus.
- Bachelor's degree in Statistics, Economics, Mathematics or a related field.
