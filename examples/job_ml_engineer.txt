Machine Learning Engineer - NLP (fictional example posting)

About the role
We are looking for a Machine Learning Engineer to build and deploy NLP models that power our customer support platform.

Requirements
- 2+ years of experience building machine learning models in production.
- Strong Python skills and experience with deep learning frameworks such as PyTorch or TensorFlow.
- Experience with transformer models (BERT, GPT) and natural language processing.
- Experience deploying models as REST APIs with Docker and Kubernetes on AWS.
- Solid SQL skills and experience building data pipelines.
- Familiarity with MLOps tools such as MLflow and CI/CD.
- Bachelor's degree in Computer Science or a related field.

Nice to have
- Experience with large language models and retrieval augmented generation.
- Strong communication skills and experience mentoring others.

We offer competitive salary, health insurance and paid time off. We are an equal opportunity employer.
